# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Product Owner

### Role Summary
The Technical Product Owner bridges the gap between business requirements and technical implementation. They translate business needs into technical specifications, review implementation feasibility, and guide technical decisions to ensure solutions are both business-aligned and technically sound.

### Responsibilities
- Translate business requirements into detailed technical specifications
- Review proposed technical approaches for feasibility and best practices
- Guide technical decisions on architecture, technology stack, and design patterns
- Collaborate with developers to identify technical constraints and opportunities
- Ensure technical solutions align with long-term product vision
- Participate in technical design reviews and provide feedback

### Goals
- Enable efficient translation between business and technical domains
- Reduce rework due to misaligned technical and business expectations
- Support sustainable, maintainable technical implementations
- Facilitate knowledge sharing between technical and non-technical stakeholders

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Coordinates on timeline and resource impacts of technical decisions
- **Developers**: Provides technical guidance, reviews code, and helps resolve technical challenges
- **Product Managers**: Collaborates on feature specs and ensures technical feasibility of roadmap items
- **Stakeholder Communication Lead**: Provides technical context for stakeholder communications

### Decision Authority
- Technical approach and implementation strategy
- Technology stack recommendations
- Technical trade-offs and risk mitigation strategies

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and specifications
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Technical review meetings and design sessions
- Code review feedback and technical guidance

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Release Manager owns the end-to-end release planning, coordination, and execution process. They ensure releases are well-planned, thoroughly validated, and successfully deployed while maintaining clear communication with all stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain release plans and deployment schedules
- Coordinate release readiness activities across teams
- Manage release documentation, notes, and deployment procedures
- Ensure quality gates are met before proceeding with deployment
- Coordinate with QA on testing completion and defect resolution
- Manage deployment execution and monitor post-deployment stability
- Track and communicate release status to stakeholders

### Goals
- Execute releases on schedule with minimal risk and downtime
- Ensure all teams are aligned on release scope and timing
- Maintain clear documentation of what is being released and why
- Support rapid rollback procedures if issues occur post-deployment

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Quality Assurance Lead**: Validates release readiness and testing completion
- **Developers**: Coordinates build preparation and deployment support
- **Project Manager**: Aligns on release timeline and manages scope decisions
- **Stakeholder Communication Lead**: Provides release status and deployment notifications

### Decision Authority
- Release schedule and deployment timing
- Go/no-go decisions based on quality and readiness
- Deployment procedures and rollback strategies

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and deployment runbooks
- Release notes and what's new documentation
- Deployment status updates and incident communications
- Post-deployment retrospectives

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Lead defines and oversees the testing strategy, maintains quality standards, and ensures that delivered software meets defined acceptance criteria and performs reliably in production.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and approach for each release cycle
- Establish and maintain quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Plan and coordinate testing activities across the team
- Manage defect tracking, prioritization, and resolution workflow
- Oversee test coverage, automation, and reporting
- Validate quality gates before release approval
- Identify quality trends and recommend process improvements

### Goals
- Ensure delivered software meets all acceptance criteria
- Minimize defects reaching production
- Maintain consistent, high quality standards
- Enable continuous improvement of testing processes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborates on test planning and coordinates test execution
- **Release Manager**: Validates quality readiness and provides release approval
- **Project Manager**: Escalates quality risks and assists with defect prioritization
- **Technical Product Owner**: Aligns on acceptance criteria and technical quality standards

### Decision Authority
- Quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Testing methodologies and test case design
- Quality gates and release readiness decisions

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Defect reports and quality metrics
- Quality gate assessments and release readiness reviews
- Quality improvement recommendations

---

## Stakeholder Communication Lead

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Communication Lead manages all communication with stakeholders, gathers feedback, and ensures stakeholders are kept informed of project status, risks, and significant milestones. They serve as the primary point of contact for non-project-team stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain stakeholder communication strategy
- Create and distribute regular status updates and progress reports
- Manage stakeholder feedback collection and escalation
- Communicate project risks, issues, and mitigation strategies
- Coordinate announcements of major milestones and releases
- Maintain stakeholder engagement and satisfaction
- Identify and address stakeholder concerns and questions

### Goals
- Keep stakeholders informed and engaged throughout the project
- Build trust and confidence in project delivery
- Enable informed decision-making by stakeholders
- Reduce surprises and miscommunications

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Receives project status and key information for communication
- **Technical Product Owner**: Provides technical context for stakeholder communications
- **Release Manager**: Communicates release updates and deployment notifications
- **Quality Assurance Lead**: Communicates quality status and testing progress

### Decision Authority
- Communication frequency, channels, and distribution lists
- Message prioritization and emphasis
- Response timing for stakeholder inquiries

### Typical Communication
- Status reports and dashboard updates
- Stakeholder briefings and town halls
- Announcement communications
- Feedback collection and survey administration

---

## Resource Manager

### Role Summary
The Resource Manager plans and optimizes resource allocation across projects and initiatives. They manage team capacity, identify resource conflicts, and ensure teams are appropriately staffed to meet project commitments.

### Responsibilities
- Assess resource requirements for projects and initiatives
- Plan resource allocation based on availability and skill needs
- Monitor team capacity and workload across projects
- Identify and resolve resource conflicts and constraints
- Support team member development and growth
- Manage resource scheduling and capacity planning
- Escalate resource constraints and conflicts to leadership

### Goals
- Optimize resource utilization across projects
- Ensure projects have appropriate staffing and skills
- Support team member career growth and satisfaction
- Minimize impact of resource constraints on delivery

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Provides resource requirements and participates in capacity planning
- **Developers**: Provides staffing recommendations and career development support
- **Product Managers**: Collaborates on resource planning for roadmap items
- **Executive Sponsor/Leadership**: Escalates resource conflicts and constraints

### Decision Authority
- Resource allocation and assignment across projects
- Team composition and skill matching
- Resource conflict resolution

### Typical Communication
- Capacity planning and resource forecasting
- Staffing recommendations and assignments
- Resource conflict escalations
- Team development and growth planning
